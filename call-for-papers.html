Call for Papers & Guidelines

Inviting original empirical, conceptual, and practice-oriented research papers addressing the future of digital and sustainable business management.

Author Invitation

Invitation to Researchers, Academicians & Industry Practitioners

The Organizing Committee invites academicians, researchers, management professionals, industry practitioners, and students to submit original research papers and abstracts addressing the conference theme: "Digital Transformation and Sustainability in Business Management: Emerging Trends, Innovations, and Future Perspectives."

Submissions should contribute fresh conceptual frameworks, empirical findings, experimental analytics, or case studies that explore the intersection of frontier technology, corporate strategy, sustainability, and responsible leadership.

Submission Principles

  • Papers must represent original work that has not been previously published or under review elsewhere.
  • Submissions must clearly identify the relevant Conference Track (Track 1 to Track 6) and sub-theme.
  • Author names, institutional affiliations, designations, and contact emails should be specified on the title page.
  • All submissions will undergo double-blind peer review by designated academic referees.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present at the conference.
  • Registered participants and presenting authors will receive official certificates according to conference rules.

Manuscript Specifications

Abstract Length: 200 – 300 Words
Keywords: 3 – 5 Keywords
Font & Typography: Times New Roman (Title: 14pt bold, Abstract Text: 12pt regular)
Submission Mode: Abstract submission via official Google Form / portal [TO BE CONFIRMED]

Quality Assurance

7-Step Peer Review Process

Ensuring academic rigor, research integrity, and constructive evaluation for all submitted manuscripts.

Step 01

Initial Screening

Verification of scope alignment, manuscript completeness, and preliminary originality checks.

Step 02

Double-Blind Peer Review

Evaluation by independent subject-matter experts without author or reviewer identification.

Step 03

Editorial Evaluation

Synthesis of referee assessments by the Track Chairs and Editorial Board.

Step 04

Author Feedback & Revision

Reviewer commentary communicated to authors for mandatory revisions if recommended.

Step 05

Final Acceptance Decision

Formal issuance of acceptance letter upon satisfactory addressal of revision points.

Step 06

Camera-Ready Submission

Submission of finalized camera-ready manuscript adhering to conference layout templates.

Step 07

Publication & Presentation Inclusion

Scheduling in the official conference program and consideration for publication partners, proceedings with ISBN, or indexed partner journals following committee ratification.

Academic Integrity & AI-Use Ethics Statement

The conference strictly enforces ethical standards of scholarship. All submissions will be checked for plagiarism through standard similarity detection software. Submissions exceeding institutional similarity thresholds (excluding references and quotes) will not be accepted.

Authors utilizing Generative AI tools in any phase of research, data analysis, or manuscript preparation must transparently disclose the tools used, the nature of assistance, and uphold sole responsibility for all factual claims, data integrity, and citations.
