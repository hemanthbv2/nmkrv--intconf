4. ABOUT TAB
About the Conference
The Department of Management, NMKRV College, Bengaluru, proudly hosts the International 
Management Conference 2027, bringing together academicians, researchers, management 
professionals, industry leaders and students to examine the changing landscape of business and 
management in an increasingly digital and sustainability-conscious world.
The conference provides a platform for presenting original research, sharing innovative 
practices, discussing emerging management challenges and fostering collaboration across 
business, industry and academia. It focuses on how digital transformation, artificial

intelligence, data analytics, sustainability and responsible management are reshaping 
organizations, markets, finance, human resources and the future of work.
Conference Objectives

Provide an international platform for management scholars, researchers, practitioners and 
students.

Explore emerging trends in digital transformation and sustainable business management.

Encourage interdisciplinary and industry-academia collaboration.

Share evidence-based management practices, innovations and policy perspectives.

Identify future research directions and practical solutions for resilient and responsible 
organizations.
About NMKRV College
Established in 1973, NMKRV College was founded in the cherished memory of Sri. Meda 
Kasturiranga Setty and Smt. Nagarathnamma Meda Kasturiranga Setty, inspired by the vision 
that educating a girl child is like educating an entire family. Managed by the RV Educational 
Institutions, the college has been a centre of academic excellence and empowerment for over five 
decades.
Beginning with the 2025–26 academic year, NMKRV College became a co-educational institution, 
marking a new chapter of inclusivity while upholding its rich legacy of excellence.
The college offers diverse programmes across the humanities, sciences, commerce, management, 
computer science, computer applications, and languages. Situated in the heart of Bengaluru, India’s 
innovation and knowledge hub, NMKRV College combines academic excellence with rich industry 
exposure and cultural opportunities, nurturing responsible leaders, compassionate citizens, and 
catalysts for positive social change.

7. PEER REVIEW PROCESS
1. Initial Screening
Check scope, formatting, academic integrity and completeness.
2. Reviewer Assignment
Assign suitable independent reviewers; use double-blind review if formally adopted.
3. Review Criteria
Originality, relevance, theoretical/conceptual contribution, methodology, data analysis, 
significance, clarity and references.
4. Review Recommendation
Accept; Accept with Minor Revisions; Major Revisions; Reject.
5. Editorial / Committee Decision
Consider reviewer recommendations and issue the final decision.
6. Revision Process
Authors submit revised papers and responses where revisions are requested.
7. Final Acceptance
Confirm academic, ethical, formatting and conference requirements are satisfied.
