About the Conference & Institution

Academic excellence, pioneering management education, and over five decades of transformative educational leadership under Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST).

Conference Scope

Navigating the Next Frontier in Global Business

The Department of Management, NMKRV College, Bengaluru, proudly hosts the International Management Conference 2027, bringing together academicians, researchers, management professionals, industry leaders, and students to examine the changing landscape of business and management in an increasingly digital and sustainability-conscious world.

The conference provides a dynamic platform for presenting original research, sharing innovative practices, discussing emerging management challenges, and fostering collaboration across business, industry, and academia. It focuses on how digital transformation, artificial intelligence, data analytics, sustainability, and responsible management are reshaping organizational strategies and competitive advantage.

Guiding Principles

Core Objectives of the Conference

01

Interdisciplinary Dialogue

To facilitate meaningful dialogue and exchange of ideas among researchers, practitioners, educators, and students on digital transformation and sustainable management practices.

02

Impact of Emerging Technologies

To examine the profound impact of artificial intelligence, machine learning, cloud analytics, and fintech on business models, corporate operations, and customer experience.

03

ESG & Sustainability Strategies

To explore sustainable business practices, ESG integration, circular economy frameworks, green finance, and responsible governance mechanisms across industries.

04

Industry-Academia Collaboration

To bridge the gap between academic management research and enterprise practice by addressing real-world industry challenges and digital workforce transformations.

05

Scholarly Dissemination & Best Practices

To encourage and publish high-quality conceptual and empirical research papers, case studies, and managerial perspectives that contribute meaningfully to the body of business knowledge.

Academic Heritage

About NMKRV College

Established in 1973, NMKRV College was founded in the cherished memory of Sri. Meda Kasturiranga Setty and Smt. Nagarathnamma Meda Kasturiranga Setty, inspired by the visionary belief that educating a girl child is like educating an entire family. Managed by the RV Educational Institutions (RSST), the college has stood as a beacon of academic distinction for over five decades.

Granted Autonomous status by UGC and accredited with NAAC 'A+' Grade, NMKRV has evolved into a premier institution offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral research programs. Reflecting contemporary educational needs, the institution has embraced a co-educational structure from the academic year 2025–26, broadening its academic horizon while honoring its founding values.

Organizing Department

Department of Management

The Department of Management at NMKRV College is committed to cultivating future-ready leaders equipped with critical thinking, ethical integrity, and digital business acumen. Offering comprehensive curricula in General Management, Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, and Business Analytics, the department emphasizes experiential learning through corporate internships, industry seminars, and international academic initiatives.

With a vibrant faculty roster engaged in active research and corporate consultancy, the department continuously fosters innovation, entrepreneurship, and sustainable leadership, preparing students to excel in a borderless global economy.

RV RSST

Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST)

Legacy of Educational Excellence Since 1940

Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust (RSST), the governing body of RV Educational Institutions, is one of India's most respected educational trusts. Founded in 1940 with six students by Sri M. C. Sivananda Sarma, RSST today operates over 25 premier educational institutions across Bengaluru, providing world-class education from kindergarten to doctoral research in engineering, management, humanities, health sciences, and legal studies.

Under the visionary stewardship of President Dr. M. P. Shyam and the distinguished Board of Trustees, RSST institutions continue to champion innovation, social responsibility, and international academic collaboration.
