Participation Policy

Author Registration & Certificate Entitlement

At least one author of each accepted paper must complete registration to present their paper in person or virtually and have the manuscript scheduled in the official conference proceedings.

All registered participants, presenting authors, and co-authors who register will receive official Participation & Presentation Certificates according to conference guidelines.
