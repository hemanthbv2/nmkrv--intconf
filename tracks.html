Conference Tracks & Themes

Six specialized interdisciplinary tracks addressing the future of digital and sustainable business management.

Interdisciplinary Research Scope

Six Specialized Research Tracks

Authors are encouraged to submit empirical, theoretical, or practice-oriented papers aligned with one of the following tracks.

Track 01

General Management

Sustainable Business Models & ESG Integration
  • Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) frameworks in modern enterprise strategy.
  • Circular economy practices, green supply chain operations, and carbon footprint reduction.
  • Corporate social responsibility (CSR) and ethical leadership in the digital era.
Track 02

AI and Data Analytics

Digital Transformation & Smart Operations
  • Enterprise adoption of AI, Internet of Things (IoT), and data analytics for business agility.
  • Automation, digital logistics, and cloud computing in operations management.
  • Overcoming barriers to digital adoption and managing organizational change.
Track 03

Finance

Sustainable Finance, FinTech & Risk Management
  • Green finance, sustainable investing, and climate risk assessment models.
  • Role of FinTech, Blockchain, and digital assets in modern financial ecosystems.
  • Financial inclusion, digital banking transformation, and regulatory technology (RegTech).
Track 04

Marketing

Technology-Driven Marketing & Consumer Behavior
  • Sustainable marketing strategies and consumer perception of eco-friendly brands.
  • Digital consumer insights, e-commerce dynamics, and personalized customer experience.
  • Ethics in digital advertising, data privacy, and algorithmic marketing.
Track 05

Human Resources

Strategic HR & Future of Work in the Digital Age
  • Managing remote/hybrid workforces, digital workplace culture, and employee wellbeing.
  • Continuous learning, reskilling, and talent management for sustainable growth.
  • Human-AI collaboration and leadership in tech-enabled organizations.
Track 06

Interdisciplinary & Emerging Perspectives

Digital Transformation, AI and Social Change
  • AI, Literature, Language and Digital Humanities
  • Technology, Culture, Identity and Human Values
  • Digital Media, Communication and the Future of Society
  • Technology-Enabled Education, Learning and Skill Development
  • Digital Wellbeing, Psychology and the Future of Work
  • Ethics, Privacy, Digital Citizenship and Responsible Technology
  • Digital Inclusion, Diversity, Gender and Social Justice
  • Cultural Heritage, Sustainability and Digital Preservation
  • Technology, Sustainability and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
